1. Project Overview

The Tactical X-Band Digital Twin simulates an airborne communication node (ACN) at 8.0 GHz serving sixteen registered ground terminals across mountainous, desert, plains, jungle, and maritime littoral theaters. Ground terminals include dismounted soldier EUDs and a mobile command post (MCP). Traffic enters strict-priority 3GPP 5QI FQ-CoDel queues, links are evaluated from first-principles propagation and DEM ray-marching, and a 4-channel parallel TDMA scheduler allocates uplink airtime.

3-Way Lockstep Ablation: Every simulation tick evaluates Legacy_Static_TDMA, Reactive_M_LWDF (look-ahead disabled, Φevac ≡ 1.0), and Predictive_Tac_TAD under identical terrain, trajectory, and traffic arrivals.

3. UAV Trajectory & Kinematics

Ellipse mode follows a rotated, arc-length-parameterized orbit. Closed waypoint mode validates routes, builds a cubic Hermite spline with bounded tangents, and evaluates position by arc length at the configured true airspeed (default 60 m/s, 0 m/s stationary hold). Playback speed (0.1×–10×) changes frame-request cadence only and never alters Δt = 20 ms.

4. Multi-Biome Terrain & Tile Overzoom

The baseline real-terrain raster is a 512×512 Ladakh SRTM DEM centered at 34.5°N, 76.5°E (~359.45 m × 434.17 m ground resolution), complemented by five multi-biome presets (Ladakh Alpine, Thar Arid Desert, Indo-Gangetic Plains, Northeast Tropical Jungle, Arabian Sea Littoral) plus offline SRTM .hgt and GeoTIFF ingestion.

For Web Mercator basemaps (Geo and 2D Radar SAT), remote Esri/Terrarium sources are clamped at maxNativeZoom = 15 while the camera supports Z4 through Z18 via clean parent-tile sub-quadrant cropping: at target z > 15, tile (15, x >> dz, y >> dz) is sub-cropped and scaled onto the viewport without missing-tile errors.

5. 4-Sector ITU-R F.1336 Antenna Array

The airborne relay carries four body-fixed directional quadrant antennas at boresights 0° (S0 FWD), 90° (S1 STBD), 180° (S2 AFT), and 270° (S3 PORT) relative to aircraft heading ψheading, with peak gain G0 = 16.0 dBi, 3 dB beamwidth θ3dB = 65°, and 1.5 dB crossover handover hysteresis at ±45° boundaries.

(A-10)   Gsector(θ) = G0 − min[12(θ/θ3dB)², Gsidelobe],    ψsector ∈ {0°, 90°, 180°, 270°}

6. Propagation, Diffraction & Sky Noise Coupling

Each link sums transmit power and directional antenna gain, then subtracts FSPL, multi-ridge Bullington + spherical crest diffraction (ITU-R P.526-15), altitude-integrated oxygen/water-vapor absorption (ITU-R P.676-13), rain attenuation capped at the 4,800 m freezing isotherm (ITU-R P.838-3 / P.839-4), and receiver sky-noise temperature degradation (ITU-R P.618-14). Instantaneous SNR maps to an 8-tier DVB-S2X / 3GPP Rel-17 NTN ACM table (−1.5 dB to 22.0 dB; 0.33 to 4.95 bit/s/Hz).

7. 3-Way TDMA Ablation & Predictive Tac-TAD

Each 20 ms superframe allocates 1 ms control, 3–5 ms downlink broadcast, and 16 ms uplink per quadrant (64 ms aggregate spatial-reuse uplink capacity across 4 concurrent sectors, τguard = 12 μs, minimum slot quantum 200 μs).

8. 3GPP TS 23.501 5QI Bearers & FQ-CoDel Queues

  • 5QI 69 (Emergency C2): Strict priority, 60 ms PDB, 120-packet reserved buffer ceiling, protected against shadow pruning.
  • 5QI 65 (Mission-Critical PTT Voice): High priority, 75 ms PDB.
  • 5QI 67 (Mission-Critical Real-Time Video): Medium priority, 100 ms PDB, Poisson packet arrivals.
  • 5QI 70 (Reconnaissance Photo): Background priority, 300 ms PDB; total terminal queue bounded at 400 packets.

10. v4.1.0 Benchmark Methodology & Statistical Evaluation

Paired ablation comparisons evaluate Legacy_Static_TDMA, Reactive_M_LWDF, and Predictive_Tac_TAD across identical seeds, terrain models, trajectories, and traffic profiles. Primary endpoints track delivered goodput, outage waste, delay drops vs. overflow drops by 5QI class, and worker 50 Hz step latency (p50/p95 well under the 20 ms deadline).

Correlated frames within one run are never treated as independent replicates; paired scenario/seed differences form the unit of statistical inference using paired t-intervals or Wilcoxon signed-rank / bootstrap intervals.
