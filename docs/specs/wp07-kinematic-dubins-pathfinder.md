## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

### 1.1 Context & Motivation
Airborne relay nodes (ACN tactical UAVs and manned reconnaissance aircraft) simulated in the Ladakh sector ($34.5^\circ\text{N}, 76.5^\circ\text{E}$) operate under strict aerodynamic and kinematic constraints. Unlike ground personnel or agile quadcopters, fixed-wing airframes cannot execute instantaneous heading discontinuities (e.g. sharp $90^\circ$ or $135^\circ$ waypoint corners generated by grid-based A* or Voronoi tactical pathfinders). Furthermore, fixed-wing aircraft have bounded maximum bank angles ($\phi_{\max}$) and finite aerodynamic climb/descent rate envelopes ($\dot{h}_{\max,\text{climb}}$, $\dot{h}_{\max,\text{descent}}$).

Feeding unsmoothed polyline waypoints into flight control systems or simulated RF pointing arrays introduces severe artifacts:
1. **Unbounded Centripetal Acceleration:** Corner turning radius $R \to 0$ implies required bank angle $\phi \to 90^\circ$ and infinite $g$-loading.
2. **Terrain Collision Regressions:** Naive curve smoothing (such as unrestricted cubic splines or Bezier curves) suffers from polynomial oscillation (Runge phenomenon) and corner cutting, potentially dipping below mountain ridges or penetrating 3D keep-out obstacles.
3. **Vertical Envelope Violations:** Vertical waypoint step-jumps demand aerodynamically unachievable vertical climb rates, causing stalling or ground-impact hazards.

WP-07 establishes a mathematically rigorous, 100% pure, deterministic 3D kinematic trajectory smoother and auditor based on canonical Dubins curves, tangent circular fillets, and bidirectional vertical slope bounding.

### 1.2 Zero-Duplication & Architecture Seams
- **`src/core/kinematicPathSmoother.ts`:** A standalone, pure functional engine with zero external dependencies, zero mutable state, and zero `Math.random()`. Fully decoupled from Web Worker state and DOM.
- **`src/core/tacticalAiPathfinder.ts`:** Retains 100% backward compatibility. An optional `kinematicConstraints?: FixedWingKinematicConstraints` parameter is added to pathfinding workflows; when omitted, all existing 90 test suites execute bit-for-bit identical logic.
- **50 Hz Simulation Loop Budget:** Live simulation ticks in `src/worker/simWorker.ts` run at $\Delta t = 20\text{ ms}$. Individual Dubins solves execute in $< 50\text{ µs}$; 20-waypoint trajectory smoothing and Menger-curvature auditing execute in $< 2\text{ ms}$, ensuring zero frame drops.

## 2. Mathematical Formulations & Algorithms

### 2.1 Coordinated-Turn Minimum Radius
For an aircraft traveling at true airspeed $v$ (m/s) in steady coordinated level turn with bank angle $\phi$:
$$F_{\text{centripetal}} = m \frac{v^2}{R} = m g \tan \phi$$
where $g = 9.80665\text{ m/s}^2$ is standard gravitational acceleration. The minimum permissible turn radius $R_{\min}$ is:
$$R_{\min} = \frac{v^2}{g \tan \phi_{\max}}$$

**Guards & Invariants:**
- If $v \le 0 \implies R_{\min} = 0$.
- If $\phi_{\max} \le 0 \implies R_{\min} = \infty$.
- Positive $\phi_{\max}$ is clamped to $[0.5^\circ, 85.0^\circ]$ to ensure numerical stability and prevent division by zero near $\phi \to 90^\circ$.
- All inputs are validated via `Number.isFinite`.

### 2.2 Canonical 6-Family 2D Dubins Solver

Given initial pose $(x_s, y_s, \theta_s)$ and goal pose $(x_e, y_e, \theta_e)$ with minimum turn radius $R = R_{\min}$, Dubins (1957) proved that the time-optimal path with bounded curvature $|\kappa| \le 1/R$ is composed of at most three segments selected from six canonical words:
- **CSC families:** `LSL`, `RSR`, `LSR`, `RSL` (Left/Right arcs with a straight line segment)
- **CCC families:** `RLR`, `LRL` (Three tangential circular arcs of alternating directions)

#### Heading Convention
All angles $\theta$ are represented in standard Cartesian radians counter-clockwise (CCW) from the $+x$ (East) axis:
$$\vec{v} = (v \cos \theta, v \sin \theta)$$
Standard conversion utilities to/from navigation bearing (degrees clockwise from North) are provided:
$$\theta_{\text{nav}} = \operatorname{mod360}\left(90^\circ - \theta_{\text{cartesian}} \cdot \frac{180^\circ}{\pi}\right)$$
$$\theta_{\text{cartesian}} = \left(90^\circ - \theta_{\text{nav}}\right) \cdot \frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$$

#### Center-to-Center Geometry & Word Solutions
Let the turning circle centers for start and end poses be:
$$\mathbf{C}_{s,L} = \begin{pmatrix} x_s - R \sin \theta_s \\ y_s + R \cos \theta_s \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{C}_{s,R} = \begin{pmatrix} x_s + R \sin \theta_s \\ y_s - R \cos \theta_s \end{pmatrix}$$
$$\mathbf{C}_{e,L} = \begin{pmatrix} x_e - R \sin \theta_e \\ y_e + R \cos \theta_e \end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{C}_{e,R} = \begin{pmatrix} x_e + R \sin \theta_e \\ y_e - R \cos \theta_e \end{pmatrix}$$

1. **`LSL` (Left - Straight - Left):**
   - Center vector: $\mathbf{D} = \mathbf{C}_{e,L} - \mathbf{C}_{s,L}$, distance $d = \|\mathbf{D}\|$.
   - Outer tangent length: $p = d$.
   - Tangent heading: $\psi = \operatorname{atan2}(D_y, D_x)$.
   - Arc 1 (Left): $t = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\psi - \theta_s)$.
   - Arc 2 (Left): $q = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\theta_e - \psi)$.
   - Total length: $L_{\text{LSL}} = R(t + q) + p$.

2. **`RSR` (Right - Straight - Right):**
   - Center vector: $\mathbf{D} = \mathbf{C}_{e,R} - \mathbf{C}_{s,R}$, distance $d = \|\mathbf{D}\|$.
   - Outer tangent length: $p = d$.
   - Tangent heading: $\psi = \operatorname{atan2}(D_y, D_x)$.
   - Arc 1 (Right): $t = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\theta_s - \psi)$.
   - Arc 2 (Right): $q = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\psi - \theta_e)$.
   - Total length: $L_{\text{RSR}} = R(t + q) + p$.

3. **`LSR` (Left - Straight - Right):**
   - Center vector: $\mathbf{D} = \mathbf{C}_{e,R} - \mathbf{C}_{s,L}$, distance $d = \|\mathbf{D}\|$.
   - Feasible iff $d \ge 2R$. (If $d < 2R$, discards branch).
   - Inner tangent length: $p = \sqrt{d^2 - 4 R^2}$.
   - Angle offset: $\phi = \operatorname{atan2}(2R, p)$.
   - Tangent heading: $\psi = \operatorname{atan2}(D_y, D_x) + \phi$.
   - Arc 1 (Left): $t = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\psi - \theta_s)$.
   - Arc 2 (Right): $q = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\psi - \theta_e)$.
   - Total length: $L_{\text{LSR}} = R(t + q) + p$.

4. **`RSL` (Right - Straight - Left):**
   - Center vector: $\mathbf{D} = \mathbf{C}_{e,L} - \mathbf{C}_{s,R}$, distance $d = \|\mathbf{D}\|$.
   - Feasible iff $d \ge 2R$. (If $d < 2R$, discards branch).
   - Inner tangent length: $p = \sqrt{d^2 - 4 R^2}$.
   - Angle offset: $\phi = \operatorname{atan2}(2R, p)$.
   - Tangent heading: $\psi = \operatorname{atan2}(D_y, D_x) - \phi$.
   - Arc 1 (Right): $t = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\theta_s - \psi)$.
   - Arc 2 (Left): $q = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\theta_e - \psi)$.
   - Total length: $L_{\text{RSL}} = R(t + q) + p$.

5. **`RLR` (Right - Left - Right):**
   - Center vector: $\mathbf{D} = \mathbf{C}_{e,R} - \mathbf{C}_{s,R}$, distance $d = \|\mathbf{D}\|$.
   - Feasible iff $d \le 4R$. (If $d > 4R$, intermediate circle cannot bridge centers).
   - Intermediate circle angle: $\phi = \arccos(d / (4R))$.
   - Heading to center: $\psi = \operatorname{atan2}(D_y, D_x)$.
   - Arc 1 (Right): $t = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\theta_s - \psi + \pi/2 - \phi)$.
   - Arc 2 (Left): $p = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\pi + 2\phi)$.
   - Arc 3 (Right): $q = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\theta_s - \theta_e - t + p)$.
   - Total length: $L_{\text{RLR}} = R(t + p + q)$.

6. **`LRL` (Left - Right - Left):**
   - Center vector: $\mathbf{D} = \mathbf{C}_{e,L} - \mathbf{C}_{s,L}$, distance $d = \|\mathbf{D}\|$.
   - Feasible iff $d \le 4R$.
   - Intermediate circle angle: $\phi = \arccos(d / (4R))$.
   - Heading to center: $\psi = \operatorname{atan2}(D_y, D_x)$.
   - Arc 1 (Left): $t = \operatorname{mod2pi}(-\theta_s + \psi - \pi/2 + \phi)$.
   - Arc 2 (Right): $p = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\pi + 2\phi)$.
   - Arc 3 (Left): $q = \operatorname{mod2pi}(\theta_e - \theta_s - t + p)$.
   - Total length: $L_{\text{LRL}} = R(t + p + q)$.

The solver computes all 6 candidates, discards infeasible/NaN solutions, and selects the word minimizing total arc length $L^* = \min_w L_w$. Path discretization samples equidistant arc points at spacing $\Delta s \le \text{sampleStepM}$.

### 2.3 Waypoint Polyline Inscribed Fillets & Dubins Fallback

Given a sequence of 3D waypoints $\mathbf{P}_0, \mathbf{P}_1, \dots, \mathbf{P}_{N-1}$:
For each interior corner vertex $\mathbf{P}_k$:
1. Calculate normalized unit vectors along incoming leg $\mathbf{u}_1 = (\mathbf{P}_k - \mathbf{P}_{k-1}) / L_{1}$ and outgoing leg $\mathbf{u}_2 = (\mathbf{P}_{k+1} - \mathbf{P}_k) / L_{2}$.
2. Deflection angle $\Delta\psi_k = \arccos(\operatorname{clamp}(\mathbf{u}_1 \cdot \mathbf{u}_2, -1, 1))$.
3. Inscribed circular fillet tangent setback distance:
   $$T_k = R_{\min} \tan\left(\frac{\Delta\psi_k}{2}\right)$$
4. **Transition & Overlap Guard:**
   - If adjacent setbacks exceed leg length ($T_k + T_{k+1} > L_{\text{seg}}$), or if $\Delta\psi_k \ge 150^\circ$, an inscribed fillet geometry cannot cleanly fit.
   - The smoother seamlessly falls back to the exact 6-family **`computeDubinsPath2D`** connecting the safe entry pose on leg $k-1$ to the safe exit pose on leg $k$, guaranteeing that curvature never exceeds $\kappa_{\max} = 1 / R_{\min}$.
5. **Obstacle Collision Guard:**
   - If user provides predicate `isPointSafe(x, y, z)`, every point sampled along the fillet/Dubins curve is verified.
   - If any point penetrates an obstacle or terrain boundary, the smoother safely reduces the fillet radius / setback, or reverts to the original safe waypoint vertex if no feasible collision-free arc exists, incrementing `obstacleFallbackCount` in telemetry.

### 2.4 3D Climb/Descent Rate Envelope & Terrain Floor Preservation

Fixed-wing vertical speed $\dot{h}$ is constrained by propulsion power and airframe structural limits:
$$-\dot{h}_{\max,\text{descent}} \le \dot{h} \le \dot{h}_{\max,\text{climb}}$$
For horizontal aircraft groundspeed $v_h \approx \sqrt{\max(v^2 - \dot{h}^2, 10^{-6})}$, the maximum vertical gradients are:
$$s_{\text{climb}} = \frac{\dot{h}_{\max,\text{climb}}}{v_h}, \quad s_{\text{desc}} = \frac{\dot{h}_{\max,\text{descent}}}{v_h}$$

#### Bidirectional Elevation Envelope
Over discretized path points with horizontal distance step $\Delta s_i$:
1. **Forward Pass (Descent & Climb rate limiting):**
   $$z_i^{(1)} = \min\left(z_i, z_{i-1}^{(1)} + s_{\text{climb}} \Delta s_i\right)$$
   $$z_i^{(1)} = \max\left(z_i^{(1)}, z_{i-1}^{(1)} - s_{\text{desc}} \Delta s_i\right)$$
2. **Backward Pass (Anticipatory climb initiation):**
   Aircraft must begin climbing prior to reaching high-altitude waypoints:
   $$z_i^{(2)} = \max\left(z_i^{(1)}, z_{i+1}^{(2)} - s_{\text{climb}} \Delta s_{i+1}\right)$$
3. **Terrain Floor Floor Protection:**
   At every point, elevation is strictly protected:
   $$z_i^* = \max\left(z_i^{(2)}, z_{\text{floor}, i}\right)$$
   where $z_{\text{floor}, i} = \text{terrainElevation}(x_i, y_i) + \text{minClearanceM}$.
   Smoothing **never** lowers a waypoint beneath its prescribed terrain safety buffer.

### 2.5 Independent Menger-Curvature Feasibility Auditor

To provide unassailable proof of kinematic feasibility, an independent mathematical auditor evaluates the trajectory without relying on the smoother's internal parameters:
For any three consecutive 2D path points $(P_{i-1}, P_i, P_{i+1})$:
1. Side lengths: $a = \|P_i - P_{i+1}\|$, $b = \|P_{i-1} - P_{i+1}\|$, $c = \|P_{i-1} - P_i\|$.
2. Triangle area:
   $$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| (x_i - x_{i-1})(y_{i+1} - y_{i-1}) - (y_i - y_{i-1})(x_{i+1} - x_{i-1}) \right|$$
3. Exact Menger Curvature:
   $$\kappa_i = \frac{4 \cdot \text{Area}}{a \cdot b \cdot c}$$
4. Effective Turn Radius:
   $$R_i = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{\kappa_i}, & \text{if } \kappa_i > 10^{-9} \\ \infty, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
5. Required Bank Angle:
   $$\phi_i = \arctan\left(\frac{v^2 \kappa_i}{g}\right)$$
6. Observed Vertical Velocity:
   $$\dot{h}_i = v \cdot \frac{z_{i+1} - z_i}{\sqrt{(x_{i+1} - x_i)^2 + (y_{i+1} - y_i)^2 + (z_{i+1} - z_i)^2}}$$

The trajectory is certified `isFeasible = true` if and only if:
$$\max_i \phi_i \le \phi_{\max} + \epsilon_\phi, \quad \max_i \dot{h}_i \le \dot{h}_{\max,\text{climb}} + \epsilon_h, \quad \min_i \dot{h}_i \ge -\dot{h}_{\max,\text{descent}} - \epsilon_h$$
with numerical tolerance $\epsilon_\phi = 1.0^\circ$ and $\epsilon_h = 0.1\text{ m/s}$.
